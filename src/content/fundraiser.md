This year’s fundraising dinner is an open invitation to all who are willing to serve and to support and to become part of the Body. We are grateful for the churches and volunteers already at work, and we are eager to welcome new partners who share our heart for the Gospel and our commitment to those in need. Together, as part of the body of Christ, we believe we can continue and expand the good work this ministry has been called to do.

Please join us for an evening of inspiration, connection, and celebration as we gather to support the vital work of Crossing All Borders. Guests will enjoy a delicious authentic Ukrainian dinner.  We will also have a wonderful selection of silent auction items to bid on. We'll also look back at the impact made over the past year and hear directly from our panel of partner ministry representatives sharing how we've carried Christ's love both locally and around the world. The evening will conclude with a special keynote message from Pastor Leonard Rex of Mount Zion Global Methodist Church in Garner, NC. We hope you'll join us. Doors open at 5:00 PM for silent auction browsing.
